# Design System: Arda Özan

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Study Model on the Studio Table"**

The site is an architecture studio's study model of a career. A pale grey studio table is the ground; every surface on it is a cut piece of white foamboard standing on the table with a soft, offset shadow; the name, the role and the small status tags are lettered on basswood plaques; coloured acrylic appears only where a discipline is meant. The signature object is an axonometric model built in CSS 3D: one volume per discipline, each exactly as tall as the work inside it, standing on a foam base with a basswood name plaque, a drawn work scale and a north arrow. Everything else in the system is the rest of the studio: boards to pin lists on, plaques for names, a key of acrylic chips.

The register is calm and precise, not playful. Materials are matte, colours are flat faces lit from one side, motion is a slow exponential ease-out that extrudes and lifts by millimetres. The user rejected a "too toy-like" result: this is a presentation model on a professional table, not a set of building blocks. Two themes ship. Light is the daylit studio. Dark is the same model on a dim studio table: the table and boards go charcoal, the foam base dims to a mid grey that still reads lighter than the table, and the acrylic keeps its colour. The theme follows the system setting until the visitor uses the toggle, and the choice is remembered. All copy is bilingual (TR/EN), so every label must survive Turkish lengths.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A studio-table ground with foamboard boards standing on it under soft offset shadows.
- Basswood plaques with engraved-ink lettering for names, statuses and the model key.
- Discipline acrylic: one colour per discipline, used on volumes, mass chips and filter rings only.
- Vermilion for Arda, contact actions and the interaction marks.
- Barlow lettering: semi-condensed caps for plaques and labels, regular Barlow for reading text.
- Depth by real faces (a colour darkened in steps) and lift on hover, never by glossy effects.

## Colors

A near-neutral studio palette of table, foamboard and basswood, with four saturated acrylics that only mean "discipline" and one vermilion that only means Arda.

### Primary
- **Studio Vermilion** (vermilion): the contact material. Fills the email action, the closing contact slab and the contact page's email slab; also the "all disciplines" filter ring. Text on it is white.
- **Vermilion Ink** (vermilion-ink, dark theme vermilion-ink-dark): vermilion tuned for text on the table and boards: hover colour for links and row titles, the 404 numeral, the hover arrow on project rows.

### Secondary
- **Ultramarine Acrylic** (blue): Web & Backend. White lettering.
- **Chrome Yellow Acrylic** (yellow): Computer Vision & AI. Black lettering (black).
- **Model Green Acrylic** (green): Desktop & Automation. White lettering. Also the "open to work" status dot and the "live" dot.
- **Ink Acrylic** (black): Games. Theme-invariant near-black with white lettering.
- **Basswood** (wood, dark theme wood-dark): plaques, the name plaque, and the experience-only volumes (cloud internship, project work). Lettering in **Engraved Basswood Ink** (wood-ink / wood-ink-dark).

### Neutral
- **Studio Table** (table / table-dark): the page ground, scrollbar track and the inset ground of the A/B/C drawings (at 60%).
- **Foamboard** (board / board-dark): every panel, the nav bar (at 90% with backdrop blur), filter and stack chips, the A/B/C figure.
- **Model Foam** (foam / foam-dark): the model base only. In dark it is deliberately lighter than the boards so the base still reads as white foam under dim light.
- **Drafting Ink** (ink / ink-dark): text, the active language chip, the primary project-link button.
- **Pencil Grey** (muted / muted-dark): secondary text, dates, inactive nav, scrollbar thumb.
- **Score Line** (rule / rule-dark): hairline dividers between rows and the footer rule.

### Named Rules
**The Acrylic Means Discipline Rule.** Blue, yellow, green, ink-black and basswood-as-volume are a key, not a palette. They appear only bound to a discipline (through the category classes that set the acrylic and its lettering colour): on model volumes, mass chips, filter rings, highlight bullets and the A/B/C drawings. Never use them as decoration or section colour.

**The Vermilion Is Arda Rule.** Vermilion marks the person and the way to reach him: email actions, contact slabs, and the interaction marks (focus outline, text selection, active nav bar). One vermilion slab per page at most, plus the closing contact slab.

**The Fading Acrylic Rule.** Newer work is more saturated. Project markers carry an age from 0 to 3 that sets saturation to 1, 0.72, 0.5 and 0.34. Do not encode recency any other way.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Barlow Semi Condensed 500/600/700 (with Barlow, DIN Condensed, Arial)
**Body Font:** Barlow 400/500/600 (with DIN Alternate, Helvetica Neue, Arial)

**Character:** A DIN-like architectural lettering family. The semi-condensed cut is the plaque engraver: caps, slightly tracked, for labels; tight and bold for titles. Regular Barlow carries reading text at a generous 1.0625rem with relaxed leading.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, clamp(2.75rem, 7vw, 5.5rem), 1.02, -0.01em): page headers. Variants: project title (clamp(2.75rem, 6.5vw, 5.25rem)), contact slab heading (clamp(2.5rem, 6vw, 4.5rem)), the contact-page email sized to its slab (min(8.2cqi, 4.75rem)), and the 404 numeral (clamp(5rem, 18vw, 11rem), tabular).
- **Plaque Name** (700 caps, max(1.6rem, 0.72 model units)): Arda's name on the model plaque; scales with the model.
- **Lede** (600, clamp(1.6rem, 2.3vw, 2.1rem), max 22ch): the first-viewport introduction beside the model.
- **Headline** (700, 1.5rem rising to 1.875rem at 640px): section titles; year headings on the projects board (1.5rem, tabular).
- **Title** (700, 1.25rem rising to 1.4rem): project row titles, Neuvikon rows, detail block titles. 1.125rem for experience entries and prev/next (rising to 1.5rem).
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.625): all reading text, max 68ch in detail blocks. Larger tiers: 1.3rem/500 for the About lead, 1.15rem for summaries and the Neuvikon description, 1.05rem for the home intro (max 46ch). 0.95rem/500 for inline links and socials; 0.875rem for meta lines, dates and counts.
- **Label** (600 caps, 0.06em): nav 0.82rem (0.74rem under 360px), filter and model-key labels 0.8rem, definition terms 0.78rem, language chips and footer 0.75rem, project facts 0.72rem, drawing captions 0.7rem, plaque tags 0.68rem. On the model plaque: role line max(12px, 0.32u), work scale max(10px, 0.24u). Volume top faces: 0.3 model units, line height 1.05.

### Named Rules
**The Plaque Lettering Rule.** Caps are for things a model maker would engrave: names, labels, keys, scales. Paragraphs, summaries and links stay in sentence-case regular Barlow.

**The Counted Figures Rule.** Years, counts, dates and scale numbers use tabular figures.

## Layout

A 12-column grid on desktop (24px gutters), 6 on tablet from 768px (20px gutters), 4 on phones (16px gutters). Content sits in a container capped at 90rem with 20/32/48px side margins at phone/640px/1024px. The root size steps from 16px to 17px at 1600px and 18px at 2200px. Sections are separated by 64px (96px from 640px); the closing contact slab sits 80px (112px) below the last section; the footer 96px (128px) below that.

The first viewport on desktop gives the model eight columns and the introduction four, filling the screen height minus the nav. Below 1024px the model centres above the introduction, the name plaque drops below the model, and the model key (a plaque list, 1 column, 2 from 480px, 3 from 768px) carries the discipline links. Lists live on boards as rows split by score lines, laid out on the same grid: date, title, description, arrow.

### Named Rules
**The True Height Rule.** Model heights are honest: one piece of work is 0.55 model units of height, and the work scale on the plaque is drawn from the same unit. Never exaggerate a volume for effect.

**The One Unit Rule.** The model, its lettering and its plaque share one unit (clamp(15px, 6.1vw, 27px); from 1024px min(3.55vw, 6.4svh, 66px)). Size anything attached to the model in that unit.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is physical. Objects stand on the table and cast a soft shadow offset downward; on hover or focus they lift 3px and the shadow grows. In dark theme the same shadows deepen to near-black and gain a faint top highlight. Inside the model, depth comes from real faces: each volume's sides are its acrylic mixed with black in OKLab (west 12%, front 22%, back 30%, east 36%), the foam base has two darkened edges (18% and 30%), and the base casts a single soft shadow in model units.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Standing Board** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 0 rgb(0 0 0 / 0.04), 0 14px 28px -16px rgb(40 36 30 / 0.35)`; dark `0 1px 0 rgb(255 255 255 / 0.03), 0 16px 30px -16px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.7)`): every board, plaque, chip, button, slab and the nav bar at rest.
- **Lifted Board** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 0 rgb(0 0 0 / 0.04), 0 22px 36px -18px rgb(40 36 30 / 0.45)`; dark `0 1px 0 rgb(255 255 255 / 0.04), 0 24px 40px -18px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.8)`): hover or focus on liftable objects, and the selected filter chip.
- **Plaque Edge** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px` basswood darkened 20%, plus Standing Board): the cut edge of every plaque, over a top-to-bottom gradient from basswood lightened 8%.

### Named Rules
**The Standing Object Rule.** Every surface is an object on the table: it carries the Standing Board shadow and rises 3px when it is interactive and hovered. No hard offset shadows, no glows, no glossy highlights.

**The Lit Faces Rule.** A volume's sides are its own colour darkened in fixed steps. Never gradients, outlines or bevels on volumes.

## Shapes

Cut-board corners: barely softened, as if trimmed with a blade. Boards, slabs and buttons 3px; plaques, the model base, language chips and drawing grounds 2px; highlight bullets 1px. Fully round shapes are reserved for status dots, bullet points, the nav's active bar and the A/B/C callout circles. Neuvikon app icons keep their own 8px radius because they are external product marks. Small iconography is drawn, not glyph-based: the mass chip (a three-face isometric block), the north arrow, the half-filled theme square and line arrows, all inline SVG.

## Components

### Buttons
Cut boards that lift.
- **Shape:** 3px corners (2px for the white button on vermilion).
- **Contact action:** vermilion with white semibold text, 14px by 16px, email left and an arrow right that slides 4px on hover.
- **Project link:** first link ink on board text, others foamboard with ink text; 10px by 16px, semibold, outward arrow.
- **Hover / Focus:** lift 3px with Lifted Board over 0.45s ease-out-expo. Focus is a 2px vermilion outline offset 3px.

### Chips
- **Filter chip:** a foamboard chip with a mass chip, label caps and a tabular count. Selected chips stay lifted with a 2px ring in their discipline acrylic (vermilion for "all").
- **Stack chip:** foamboard, 0.875rem/500, 4px by 10px.
- **Plaque tag:** a small basswood plaque in 0.68rem caps for internship and volunteer roles.
- **Status dot:** green (open to work, live) or yellow (in development), round, with a translucent halo on the home status.

### Cards / Containers
- **Board:** foamboard, 3px corners, Standing Board shadow, 16px side padding (24px from 640px). Holds row lists divided by score lines; never a grid of equal cards.
- **Contact slab:** vermilion, 3px, white text, 24/40/48px padding, a white 60% rule above the email.
- **Drawing figure (views A, B, C):** a board holding three square drawings on a 60% table ground (plan, elevation, axonometric) in the project's acrylic, each captioned by a circled letter, with a fact list below.

### Navigation
A sticky foamboard bar at 90% opacity with backdrop blur and the Standing Board shadow. Arda's name is a small basswood plaque on the left. Links are 0.82rem caps in pencil grey, ink on hover and when active; the active link carries a 3px vermilion bar that scales in from the centre. Theme toggle (half-filled square, rotates 180 degrees on hover) and TR/EN chips (active: ink on board, 2px corners) share the name's row on phones.

### Project Row
The project index unit: a mass chip in the discipline acrylic (desaturated by age) that rises 4px on hover, the title in bold sentence-case title type that turns vermilion-ink on hover, category and meta in 0.875rem muted, the summary in muted body, and on desktop a vermilion arrow that slides in.

### Career Model (signature)
An axonometric foam base (rotateX 58 degrees, rotateZ -38 degrees) holding six linked volumes: web, vision, desktop, games, cloud and project work. Each volume's top face is its acrylic, lettered in label caps at its front-right corner. On load the volumes extrude from zero height over 1.1s with an exponential ease-out, staggered 110ms from 250ms. Hover or focus raises a volume 0.35 units and switches the name plaque to that volume's chip, name and count; focus also draws a 3px vermilion outline on the top face. The basswood plaque sits at the base's front-right corner on desktop with the work scale (notches at 0, 1, 2, 4, 6) and the north arrow, and drops below the model on smaller screens. Under reduced motion every animation and transition is removed and the model is shown fully built.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** stand every surface on the table with the Standing Board shadow and lift interactive ones 3px with Lifted Board.
- **Do** bind discipline colour through the category classes so the acrylic and its lettering colour always travel together.
- **Do** keep volume heights and the work scale on the same unit: 0.55 units per piece of work.
- **Do** letter names, labels, keys and scales in semi-condensed caps with 0.06em tracking, and keep reading text in regular Barlow.
- **Do** use the exponential ease-out (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)) for extrusion, lift and arrow slides, and remove all motion under reduced motion.
- **Do** check both themes: dark is the same model on a dim table, with the foam base kept lighter than the table.

### Don't:
- **Don't** make it toy-like: no bouncy or springy easing, no glossy plastic, no outlines or bevels on volumes, no rounded blocks.
- **Don't** use the discipline acrylics for decoration, backgrounds or section colour.
- **Don't** put more than one vermilion slab on a page besides the closing contact slab.
- **Don't** fall back to a headline over a grid of equal cards; lists are rows on boards, disciplines are volumes.
- **Don't** round corners beyond 3px on boards, plaques or buttons.
- **Don't** use hard offset shadows, glows or glyph icons; draw small marks as inline SVG.
